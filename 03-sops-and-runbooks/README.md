# Standard Operating Procedures & Runbooks

Technical procedures written for day-to-day help desk work. Each one is written
to be followed by someone who has not done the task before — clear steps, stated
prerequisites, and notes on what to do when the normal path fails.

I write these for two audiences. Technician-facing procedures assume access to
administrative tools and use precise terminology. User-facing procedures assume
no technical background and avoid jargon entirely.

## Why This Section Exists

A large share of support work is communication, not repair. The fix is often
simple; explaining it to a frustrated user, or documenting it so the next
technician does not rediscover it, is the part that takes skill. These
documents are where I practice that.

Every procedure here follows the same structure: purpose, scope,
prerequisites, numbered steps, verification, escalation criteria, and a
revision history. Consistent structure means a technician can find what they
need without reading the whole document.
